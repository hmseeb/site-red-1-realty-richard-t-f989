How I help

What you actually get when you work with Rick

Every one of these starts the same way — a phone call where you tell me what you need and I tell you honestly what it will take.

You walk into a home that fits your life — not whatever happened to be listed.

Home buying, start to keys

I listen to what you need first: bedrooms, commute, school district, budget, the dog, the stairs. Then we tour real options across Tipp City, Vandalia, Huber Heights, New Carlisle and the surrounding Miami County and Dayton-area towns until one of them is clearly yours. I handle the offer, the negotiation, the inspection follow-up and the paperwork so your closing day is uneventful — in the good way.

  1. 1 A call to hear what you are looking for and what you want to spend
  2. 2 Showings on homes that genuinely match — no time wasted
  3. 3 Offer written, negotiated and tracked to the closing table

Your first home stops feeling out of reach.

First-time buyers

If you have never bought before, the hardest part is not finding the house — it is knowing what happens next. I explain each step in plain English, tell you what the costs really are, and connect you with lenders, inspectors and title professionals who will coach you through it instead of rushing you. Low-money-down options get put on the table when they fit.

A property that earns while you own it.

Investment & income property

House hacking, a rental unit, or a home with an income side to it — we look at the numbers before the finishes. I help you weigh what a property should cash flow, what the repairs will realistically cost, and whether the deal still works once the romance of the listing photos wears off.

Your house sells, and you know what is happening the whole time.

Listing & selling your home

We price it against what is actually selling nearby, agree on what is worth fixing first, get it in front of real buyers, and I keep you updated on showings and feedback. When offers come in, we go through them line by line — price, terms, timing — so you choose with your eyes open.

A place to land when the move cannot wait.

Relocating & finding somewhere to live

Life changes faster than the market does. If you need to be somewhere by a certain date, or you are sorting out a family member's property and your own housing at the same time, call me. I have helped neighbors find a decent place at a decent price when the clock was running, and I will tell you up front what is possible.
